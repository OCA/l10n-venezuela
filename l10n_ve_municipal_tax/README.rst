This addon estimates the Venezuelan municipal economic activities tax
from configured gross-income accounts and creates a draft monthly
provision entry.

Municipal rules vary by ordinance. This addon provides a configurable
accounting estimate, not a tax return or a substitute for reviewing the
applicable ordinance.

Configuration
=============

In **Invoicing > Configuration > Settings**, configure the municipality,
rate, monthly minimum, taxable income accounts, expense account, payable
account, and miscellaneous journal.

If the minimum is expressed in MMV units, also configure the TCMMV in
VES and a real VES currency rate effective on or before the end of the
period.

Validate these parameters against the ordinance that applies to the
company.

Usage
=====

1. Open **Invoicing > Municipal Tax (VE)**.
2. Select the company and period.
3. Compute the taxable base and estimated tax.
4. Generate the draft provision entry.

Only accounting administrators can use the wizard.
