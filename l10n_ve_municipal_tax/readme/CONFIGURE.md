In **Invoicing > Configuration > Municipalities (VE)**, add the municipalities
where your companies pay the tax.

In **Invoicing > Configuration > Settings**, configure the municipality, rate,
monthly minimum, taxable income accounts, expense account, payable account, and
miscellaneous journal.

Optionally, configure the municipal withholdings suffered account: the asset
account where you book the municipal tax withheld by your customers. Its posted
balance is deducted from each monthly provision.

If the minimum is expressed in MMV units, also configure the TCMMV in VES and a
real VES currency rate effective on or before the end of the period.

Validate these parameters against the ordinance that applies to the company.
