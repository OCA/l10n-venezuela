1. In Accounting settings, configure the liability account used for ISLR
   withholdings payable.
2. In Accounting > Configuration > Tax Unit, maintain each official tax unit
   value and its effective date.
3. Maintain a VES exchange rate for every withholding and export date when the
   company or payment currency is not VES.
4. On each vendor, select the ISLR person type and default withholding concept.

Vendors without a withholding concept do not generate an ISLR withholding or
voucher.
