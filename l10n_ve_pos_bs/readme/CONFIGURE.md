Activate the VES currency and configure its current exchange rate. This addon is
only applicable when both the company and POS use a currency other than VES.

On a POS payment method that uses a bank journal in the POS currency:

* Enable **Payment in Bolivars**.
* Optionally enable **Require Transaction Reference**.
* Set a reconcilable outstanding receipts account.

The addon deliberately rejects VES tenders configured with cash journals.
