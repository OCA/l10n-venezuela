Support for configurable IGTF on customer payments for Venezuela.

The module calculates IGTF in payment and company currencies and limits
it to the invoice's available IGTF amount. Special taxpayers can accrue
IGTF on invoices in configured currencies or apply it only on payments.

Use Cases / Context
===================

Venezuelan special taxpayers must identify configured foreign-currency
customer payments subject to IGTF and keep the tax amount separate from
the invoice principal and any customer advance.

Usage
=====

To apply IGTF:

1. Configure the IGTF percentage, account, and applicable currencies.
2. Ensure the company is a Venezuelan special taxpayer.
3. Open a customer invoice and select **Register Payment**.
4. Choose an applicable payment currency and confirm the IGTF values.
5. Create the payment.

The resulting payment separates IGTF from the invoice principal.
