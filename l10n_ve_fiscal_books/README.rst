This addon generates the Venezuelan VAT sales and purchase books
required by articles 70 through 78 of the VAT Law Regulations. XLSX
amounts are expressed in VES using the exchange rate applicable on each
document date.

It provides:

- Detailed sales and purchase books with 8%, 16%, and combined 31% VAT
  columns.
- Sales separation by the emission medium captured on each posted
  document.
- Daily POS summaries for non-taxpayer sales and document-level
  contingency sales.
- Authorized fiscal-paper batches, range validation, duplicate
  detection, and low-paper warnings.
- Optional VAT withholding columns when a compatible integration is
  installed.

The addon relies on ``l10n_ve_fiscal_document`` for control numbers,
emission media, posted-document snapshots, and fiscal-data immutability.

Configuration
=============

Configure an emission medium on each Venezuelan sales journal before
posting customer documents.

For POS daily summaries, set the fiscal machine registration number in
the point of sale settings.

Register authorized contingency booklets or free-form paper under
**Accounting > Configuration > Venezuelan Fiscal Paper** when
paper-number validation is needed.

Usage
=====

Open **Accounting > Reporting > Venezuelan Fiscal Books**, choose the
sales or purchase book and a date range, and select **Generate**.
Download the generated XLSX file from the wizard.

The sales book lists invoiced POS orders by fiscal document and
summarizes only closed-session, non-invoiced POS orders by day.
Contingency POS orders are listed individually with their preprinted
invoice and control numbers.
