To register an advance:

1. Configure the customer and vendor advance accounts on the company or contact.
2. Create an inbound customer payment or an outbound vendor payment without
   selecting an invoice.
3. Post the payment.

To apply an advance:

1. Open a posted invoice or vendor bill for the same contact.
2. Select an available advance from the advances widget.
3. Enter an amount no greater than the advance balance or document residual.
4. Confirm the application.

When registering a payment greater than the document residual, select
**Keep as advance** to post the difference to the corresponding advance
account.
