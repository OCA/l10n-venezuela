This module manages customer and vendor advances through payments.

It supports company-wide advance accounts, partner-specific overrides,
overpayments retained as advances, and applying available advances to
invoices and vendor bills.

Use Cases / Context
===================

Customer receipts and vendor payments made before an invoice exists must
remain identifiable as advances instead of being mixed with ordinary
receivable or payable balances.

This module keeps those balances in dedicated accounts and later applies
them to the corresponding commercial documents while preserving partner,
multi-company, and currency traceability.

Usage
=====

To register an advance:

1. Configure the customer and vendor advance accounts on the company or
   contact.
2. Create an inbound customer payment or an outbound vendor payment
   without selecting an invoice.
3. Post the payment.

To apply an advance:

1. Open a posted invoice or vendor bill for the same contact.
2. Select an available advance from the advances widget.
3. Enter an amount no greater than the advance balance or document
   residual.
4. Confirm the application.

When registering a payment greater than the document residual, select
**Keep as advance** to post the difference to the corresponding advance
account.
