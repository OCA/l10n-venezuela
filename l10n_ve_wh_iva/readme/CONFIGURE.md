1. Open Accounting > Configuration > Settings.
2. In **Venezuela Localization - VAT Withholding**, enable **Special Taxpayer
   (SPE)** when the company has been designated by SENIAT and set the effective
   designation date.
3. Configure the liability account for VAT withheld from vendors.
4. Configure the asset account for VAT withholding vouchers received from
   customers.
5. On each partner's Accounting tab, set the taxpayer type and VAT withholding
   percentage.

The company and partners must have their Venezuelan tax identification number in
the Tax ID field before legal vouchers can be issued or exported.
