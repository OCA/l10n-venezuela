This addon manages Venezuelan VAT withholding under Administrative
Ruling SNAT/2025/000054 in both payment directions.

When a customer acting as a special taxpayer withholds VAT, the received
voucher number and amount are recorded during payment registration. The
withheld amount is posted to the configured receivable withholding
account.

When the company is designated as a special taxpayer, vendor payments
calculate the applicable 75% or 100% VAT withholding. The addon creates
the accounting write-off and a legal voucher with a monthly,
company-specific sequence. It also provides the voucher PDF and the
16-column, tab-delimited TXT export for Form 99035.

The supplier control number is included when another installed addon
provides the optional ``l10n_ve_control_number`` field. This addon does
not require that field or any unreleased localization addon.

Configuration
=============

1. Open Accounting > Configuration > Settings.
2. In **Venezuela Localization - VAT Withholding**, enable **Special
   Taxpayer (SPE)** when the company has been designated by SENIAT and
   set the effective designation date.
3. Configure the liability account for VAT withheld from vendors.
4. Configure the asset account for VAT withholding vouchers received
   from customers.
5. On each partner's Accounting tab, set the taxpayer type and VAT
   withholding percentage.

The company and partners must have their Venezuelan tax identification
number in the Tax ID field before legal vouchers can be issued or
exported.

Usage
=====

For VAT withheld by a customer, register the customer payment and enter
the withheld VAT amount and the 14-digit voucher number. The payment
posts the net cash amount and the withholding write-off.

For VAT withheld from a vendor, register payment for a posted vendor
bill. The wizard calculates the withholding from the partner's rate and
allows the amount to be adjusted. Confirming the payment creates the net
payment, withholding write-off, and legal voucher.

Use Accounting > Vendors > VAT Withholding Vouchers to review and print
vouchers. Use **Export VAT Withholdings TXT (99035)** from the same menu
to generate the SENIAT file for a half-month period.
