Management Withholding ISLR Venezuelan Laws
============================================

This module manages Income Tax Withholding (Retención de ISLR) in Venezuela:
- Concepts and rates configuration (PNRE, PNNR, PJDO, PJND).
- Sustraendo (UT subtraction calculation).
- Withholding documents on vendor bills and customer invoices.
- SENIAT XML generator for the monthly informative declaration.
- Official ARC certificate and withholding voucher printouts.
