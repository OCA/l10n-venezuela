This addon manages Venezuelan income tax (ISLR) withholdings under
Decree 1,808. It provides:

- The SENIAT withholding concepts, codes, rates, and presumptive-base
  percentages for the four person types of Decree 1,808 art. 9: resident
  and non-resident individuals, domiciled and non-domiciled legal
  entities. A concept applies only to the person types for which the
  SENIAT table gives it a code: royalties, international freight, and
  insurance premiums, for instance, only to non-domiciled or
  non-resident subjects.
- Non-resident individuals: fixed rate (34 % in general) on the
  presumptive base of the concept, without subtrahend. Non-domiciled
  legal entities: either a fixed rate or the progressive Tarifa N° 2 of
  the Income Tax Law (15 % up to 2,000 tax units, 22 % up to 3,000, 34 %
  on the excess), cumulative per vendor and calendar year.
- An optional double taxation treaty rate per non-resident vendor.
- Historical tax unit (Unidad Tributaria) values used to calculate the
  applicable subtrahend.
- A withholding concept on each vendor bill line, defaulted from the
  product or, failing that, from the vendor.
- Automatic withholding when a vendor bill is posted. Decree 1,808 art.
  1 makes the withholding arise on payment or "abono en cuenta",
  whichever comes first, and posting the bill is the abono en cuenta. A
  posted withholding entry moves the withheld amount from the vendor
  payable to the configured liability account and is reconciled with the
  bill, so the residual is the net to pay.
- One withholding voucher per bill and concept, with a monthly
  company-specific sequence and a printable PDF report. Issued vouchers
  are locked.
- The monthly ``RelacionRetencionesISLR`` XML export defined by SENIAT
  technical manual version 3.1, including zero-withholding details and
  declarations with no operations.

The withholding base is the untaxed amount of the lines of each concept
(VAT excluded), taken in bolivars from the amounts booked by the bill.
The withholding is computed and rounded in bolivars and only then
converted to the bill currency, so vouchers are always expressed in VES.
Rates, subtrahend, and amounts are snapshotted on each voucher.

Vendor credit notes never issue a voucher: the SENIAT XML rejects
negative amounts and has no document type field.

Configuration
=============

1. In Accounting settings, configure the liability account used for ISLR
   withholdings payable and the miscellaneous journal of the withholding
   entries. Posting a bill subject to withholding fails until both are
   set.
2. In Accounting > Configuration > Tax Unit, maintain each official tax
   unit value and its effective date.
3. Maintain a VES exchange rate for every withholding and export date
   when neither the company nor the bill currency is VES: posting such a
   bill converts its base to bolivars and fails without the rate.
4. On each vendor, select the ISLR person type and, optionally, a
   default withholding concept. A vendor bill with at least one line
   carrying a withholding concept cannot be posted unless the vendor has
   a valid RIF: one letter V, E, J, P, or G, eight digits, and the
   SENIAT modulo-11 check digit (hyphens and spaces are ignored). The
   normalized 10-character value is the one written in ``RifRetenido``.
   Bills without a concept are not checked.
5. On products, optionally set the withholding concept; it takes
   precedence over the vendor's.
6. On each concept, maintain the SENIAT code, rate, and taxable base
   percentage of each person type (100 when the base is not presumptive)
   and, for non-domiciled legal entities, whether the concept uses a
   fixed rate or Tarifa N° 2. The Tarifa N° 2 brackets are the
   ``TARIFA2_BRACKETS`` constant of ``models/islr_concept.py``, in tax
   units. A concept applies to a person type only when it has a SENIAT
   code for it (not ``000`` and not empty); the concept list and form
   show the person types it applies to.
7. On a non-resident individual or non-domiciled legal entity covered by
   a double taxation treaty, enable the treaty, set its rate (0 to 100,
   0 allowed) and the reference of the fiscal residence certificate.
   Non-resident vendors need the RIF assigned by SENIAT like any other
   vendor.

Lines without a withholding concept do not generate an ISLR withholding
or voucher. Invoicing users can read concepts, tax units, and vouchers,
and post bills that issue vouchers; only accounting managers can change
concepts and tax units or manage vouchers.

Usage
=====

On a vendor bill, each line shows its ISLR concept, proposed from the
product or the vendor; change or clear it while the bill is in draft.
Lines without a concept are not withheld. A bill whose line carries a
concept that does not apply to the person type of the vendor (no SENIAT
code for that type, for example royalties on a domiciled company or
national freight on a non-domiciled one) cannot be posted: the error
names the concept, the vendor, and the person type. The same check
applies to the "Crear retención de ISLR" button, to the net voucher
re-issued by a credit note, and to issuing a manual voucher. Each
voucher keeps the SENIAT code it was issued with, and the XML declares
that code even if the concept changes later.

Posting the bill issues one voucher per concept, dated at the bill
accounting date. The tax unit and VES rate in force on that date are
used for the subtrahend of resident individuals. When the withholding is
positive, a withholding entry is posted in the configured journal and
reconciled with the bill: registering the payment afterwards pays only
the net amount. When the withholding is zero (below the subtrahend
threshold), the voucher is still issued, as required by the SENIAT
completeness rule, without an entry.

For non-residents the withholding is computed on the taxable base, the
untaxed amount times the presumptive-base percentage of the concept for
the person type (for example 30 % for technical assistance, 50 % for
technological services, 90 % for royalties):

- Non-resident individual: the fixed rate of the concept on the taxable
  base, without subtrahend.
- Non-domiciled legal entity, fixed-rate concept (for example commercial
  commissions 5 % or interest paid to foreign financial institutions
  4.95 %): the rate on the taxable base.
- Non-domiciled legal entity, Tarifa N° 2 concept: the withholding of a
  bill is Tarifa N° 2 applied to the year-to-date taxable base of the
  vendor including the bill, minus Tarifa N° 2 applied to the
  year-to-date taxable base before it. The year-to-date base adds the
  issued Tarifa N° 2 vouchers of the same commercial partner and company
  in the calendar year of the bill (cancelled vouchers do not count),
  ordered by date, bill, and concept. Both terms use the tax unit in
  force at the bill date and the difference is rounded to cents in
  bolivars. The voucher stores the taxable base and the effective rate
  (withholding over taxable base), which is the rate reported in the
  XML. Accumulating per vendor and calendar year is an assumption of
  this module: the regulation sets the tariff but not the accumulation
  period.
- Double taxation treaty on the vendor: the treaty rate replaces the
  computed one and applies to the gross amount (no presumptive base, no
  Tarifa N° 2). The voucher records that the treaty was applied and the
  certificate reference. A 0 % treaty issues the voucher without an
  entry.

When a Tarifa N° 2 voucher is inserted before others of the same vendor
and month (a backdated bill) or replaced on a net base by a same-month
credit note, the issued Tarifa N° 2 vouchers of that vendor and month
that follow it are cancelled and re-issued in order, so the result does
not depend on the order in which bills and credit notes are posted.
Vouchers of other months are never recomputed, since they may already be
declared. Resetting a bill to draft does not recompute the vouchers that
follow it until the bill is posted again.

The withholding is computed in bolivars: on a bill in another currency,
the base is the bolivar amount of its lines (the company currency
balance when the company currency is VES, or its conversion at the VES
rate of the bill date otherwise), the withholding is rounded to cents in
bolivars, and the result is converted to the bill currency for the
entry. A one-cent difference left in the foreign currency when the bill
is fully paid is settled by the standard exchange difference; the
voucher is never adjusted to absorb it.

Vendor credit notes linked to a bill (created from the bill with "Credit
Note") follow these rules:

- Dated in the same month as the bill voucher: the voucher of each
  concept of the credit note is cancelled (kept for audit, linked to the
  credit note) and its entry undone. If the net base of the concept
  (bill minus credit notes) is still positive, a new voucher is issued
  on the net base, with the rate and subtrahend applied to the net and
  the person type and treaty kept from the cancelled voucher; the XML
  then carries a single row with the bill invoice and control numbers
  and the net base. If the net is zero, nothing is declared. The bill,
  the credit note, and the new withholding entry are reconciled
  together.
- Dated in a later month: the voucher already declared is left untouched
  and no voucher or XML row is issued for the credit note.
- Not linked to a bill, linked to a bill without an issued voucher, or
  linked to a bill of another company or commercial partner: no ISLR
  effect.

The ISLR concept of a line cannot be changed once its bill or credit
note is posted; reset the document to draft to change it.

A credit note that adjusted a voucher cannot be reset to draft or
cancelled: restoring the previous voucher could reopen a period already
declared to SENIAT. Register a new vendor bill to correct it. Resetting
the bill itself to draft and posting it again issues the voucher on the
net base again.

Resetting the bill to draft or cancelling it cancels its vouchers (the
number is kept) and undoes their entries; posting it again issues new
vouchers. While its voucher is issued, the withholding entry cannot be
reset to draft, cancelled, reversed, deleted, or unreconciled from the
bill, whatever the user's rights; cancel the voucher (or reset the bill)
instead. Payments of the bill can still be unreconciled as usual. For a
bill posted before the configuration was complete, or whose voucher was
cancelled, use the "Crear retención de ISLR" button.

Manual vouchers are created in draft: their number, state, links to
bills, credit notes and withholding entries, the SENIAT code, and the
values the module computes (taxable base and its percentage, Tarifa N°
2, treaty) cannot be set or changed by users, even in draft; the number
comes only from the company sequence and issuing goes through the
"Emitir" button. An issued voucher, manual ones included, never returns
to draft: the only way out of the issued state is cancelling it, which
keeps its number. A mistaken voucher is cancelled and a new one issued.
Only draft vouchers can be deleted.

Use Accounting > Vendors > Comprobantes ISLR to review and print
vouchers. Use Accounting > Vendors > XML Retenciones ISLR (SENIAT) to
export a monthly declaration.

If another installed addon provides ``l10n_ve_control_number`` on vendor
bills, the export uses that value. Otherwise, it exports ``NA`` and does
not require that optional addon.

Voucher numbers come from the company sequence (``AAAAMM`` of the issue
date plus an 8-digit monthly sequence) and cannot be entered or changed,
with one exception: an accounting manager can register a voucher issued
outside the system by checking **Emitido fuera del sistema** when
creating it and entering its own 14-digit number in that format. That
number does not consume the sequence, must be unique per company, and
can be corrected only while the voucher is a draft.

The printed voucher shows the vendor email and the date it was delivered
to the vendor. Set **Fecha de Entrega al Proveedor** on the voucher,
even after it is issued; it is the only field that stays editable.
Without it, the printed voucher leaves a blank space to fill in by hand.
The voucher form warns when the vendor has no email; posting the bill is
not blocked.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Withholding territoriality is not inferred: a bill line is withheld
  only when it carries a concept, so the user decides which payments to
  non-residents are Venezuelan-source income.
- Gross-up (tax assumed by the payer) is not supported.
- Tarifa N° 2 vouchers of later months are not recomputed when an
  earlier bill of the same vendor changes; correct them manually if
  needed.
- Providencia Administrativa SNAT/2026/00093 sets a unified digital
  regime for withholding vouchers starting 19 April 2027; it is not
  implemented yet.
