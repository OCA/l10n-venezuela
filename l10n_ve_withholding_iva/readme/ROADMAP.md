- The withholding is computed in company currency, which is expected to be the
  bolivar (VES). With another company currency the amounts are computed and
  rounded in that currency and the TXT export converts them to bolivars at the
  document date, so the exported figures may differ by rounding from the
  bolivar amounts of the documents.
- Credit notes are matched to their bill through the reversed entry. Credit
  notes adjusting several bills at once are not split between vouchers.
- When a bill whose voucher holds partial credit notes is reset to draft and
  posted again, the new voucher covers the bill only; the credit notes are not
  re-added.
- Providencia Administrativa SNAT/2026/00093 sets a unified digital regime for
  withholding vouchers starting 19 April 2027; it is not implemented yet.
