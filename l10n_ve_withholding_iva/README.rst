This addon manages Venezuelan VAT withholding under Administrative
Ruling SNAT/2025/000054 in both directions.

When the company is designated as a special taxpayer (SPE), the
withholding arises on payment or crediting to the vendor's account,
whichever happens first (article 13). Posting the vendor bill is that
crediting, so the withholding is issued when an eligible vendor bill is
posted:

- a legal voucher with a monthly, company-specific, no-gap sequence;
- a withholding journal entry that moves the withheld VAT from the
  vendor payable to the VAT withholding liability account, reconciled
  with the document's payable line.

The withholding is computed on the bill's VAT in company currency
(bolivars) and rounded there; for bills in a foreign currency the amount
in that currency is derived from it at the bill's rate, so the voucher
ties to the bill's bolivar figures.

The document residual is then the net amount to pay, and the standard
payment wizard needs no withholding logic. Vendors that are special
taxpayers themselves are also withheld. Documents expressly excluded by
article 3 can be flagged before posting. Purchase receipts are not
subject to VAT withholding.

Vendor credit notes (article 11; article 58 of the VAT Law) never
produce a voucher with negative amounts. A credit note linked to a bill
whose voucher is issued, and dated in the same withholding period
(fortnight) as that voucher:

- cancels the voucher and its withholding entry when it brings the
  bill's net VAT to zero; nothing is reported for it;
- otherwise is added to the same voucher, with an adjustment entry that
  returns its share of the withheld VAT to the vendor payable. The TXT
  reports the bill as document type 01 and the credit note as type 03
  with the affected invoice, both with positive amounts.

A credit note of a later period, without a linked bill, or whose bill
has no issued voucher is not withheld and leaves any voucher untouched.

While a voucher is issued, its withholding and adjustment entries cannot
be reset to draft, cancelled, reversed, deleted or unreconciled by any
user; the voucher must be cancelled (or the source document reset)
first.

When a customer acting as a special taxpayer withholds VAT, the received
voucher number and amount are recorded during payment registration. The
withheld amount is posted to the configured receivable withholding
account.

The addon also provides the voucher PDF and the 16-column, tab-delimited
TXT export for Form 99035.

The supplier control number is included when another installed addon
provides the optional ``l10n_ve_control_number`` field. This addon does
not require that field or any unreleased localization addon.

Configuration
=============

1. Open Accounting > Configuration > Settings.
2. In **Venezuela Localization - VAT Withholding**, enable **Special
   Taxpayer (SPE)** when the company has been designated by SENIAT and
   set the effective designation date.
3. Configure the miscellaneous journal and the liability account used
   for VAT withheld from vendors. Posting an eligible vendor bill fails
   with an explicit error while either is missing.
4. Configure the asset account for VAT withholding vouchers received
   from customers.
5. On each partner's Accounting tab, set the taxpayer type and VAT
   withholding percentage.

The company and partners must have their Venezuelan tax identification
number in the Tax ID field before legal vouchers can be issued or
exported. Withholding as an agent only applies to companies whose fiscal
country is Venezuela.

Usage
=====

For VAT withheld from a vendor, post the vendor bill. When the company
is a special taxpayer on the bill accounting date, the vendor has a Tax
ID and a withholding percentage, the bill has VAT and it is not flagged
as excluded, posting issues the withholding voucher and its reconciled
journal entry. The bill residual becomes the net amount to pay; register
the payment as usual.

To exclude an operation under article 3, set **Excluded from VAT
withholding** in the **Other Info** tab of the draft bill before
posting.

Resetting the bill to draft or cancelling it cancels the voucher,
keeping its number, and cancels or reverses the withholding entry.
Posting it again issues a new voucher.

Voucher numbers come from the company sequence and cannot be entered or
changed, with one exception: a voucher issued outside the system can be
registered by an accounting manager by checking **Emitido fuera del
sistema** when creating it and entering its own 14-digit number
(``AAAAMM`` of its issue date plus an 8-digit sequence). That number
does not consume the company sequence, must be unique per company, and
can be corrected only while the voucher is a draft. Vouchers issued from
bills always take the sequence number. An issued voucher, manual ones
included, never returns to draft: the only way out of the issued state
is cancelling it, which keeps its number. A mistaken voucher is
cancelled and a new one issued. Only draft vouchers can be deleted.

For a vendor credit note, set the bill it corrects (as the reversal
wizard does) and post it. In the voucher's fortnight a partial credit
note is added to the voucher and a total one cancels it; in a later
fortnight it is not withheld. Resetting a partial credit note to draft
removes it from the voucher and undoes its adjustment entry; posting it
again adds it back. A credit note that cancelled a voucher cannot be
reset to draft, cancelled or reversed, as the cancelled voucher number
cannot be reused: register a new vendor bill instead. Reversing a bill
with the "modify" option (cancel and re-create) is only allowed in the
voucher's fortnight; afterwards, issue a credit note.

For bills posted before the addon was configured, use **Create VAT
withholding** on the posted bill.

For VAT withheld by a customer, register the customer payment and enter
the withheld VAT amount and the 14-digit voucher number. The payment
posts the net cash amount and the withholding write-off.

Use Accounting > Vendors > VAT Withholding Vouchers to review and print
vouchers. Use **Export VAT Withholdings TXT (99035)** from the same menu
to generate the SENIAT file for a half-month period.

The printed voucher shows the content required by article 16 of PA
SNAT/2025/000054, including the vendor email and the date the voucher
was delivered to the vendor. Set **Fecha de Entrega al Proveedor** on
the voucher, even after it is issued; it is the only field that stays
editable. Without it, the printed voucher leaves a blank space to fill
in by hand. The voucher form warns when the vendor has no email; posting
the bill is not blocked.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The withholding is computed in company currency, which is expected to
  be the bolivar (VES). With another company currency the amounts are
  computed and rounded in that currency and the TXT export converts them
  to bolivars at the document date, so the exported figures may differ
  by rounding from the bolivar amounts of the documents.
- Credit notes are matched to their bill through the reversed entry.
  Credit notes adjusting several bills at once are not split between
  vouchers.
- When a bill whose voucher holds partial credit notes is reset to draft
  and posted again, the new voucher covers the bill only; the credit
  notes are not re-added.
- Providencia Administrativa SNAT/2026/00093 sets a unified digital
  regime for withholding vouchers starting 19 April 2027; it is not
  implemented yet.
