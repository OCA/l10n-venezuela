This addon adapts the standard invoice report to Venezuelan fiscal-document
requirements. It adds the fiscal control number and assignment date, marks
exempt lines, formats the legal document date, and prints authorized printer
information.

Companies can also show document totals in a configured bolivar reference
currency using the exchange rate at the document date, and show untaxed and tax
amounts on each invoice line.

The report changes apply only to Venezuelan customer fiscal documents. Other
customer invoices and vendor documents keep the standard Odoo report output.

The legal basis covered by this addon includes Providencia Administrativa
SNAT/2011/00071 and Providencia Administrativa SNAT/2024/000102.
